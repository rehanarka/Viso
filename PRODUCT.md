# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Viso is for operations leaders, HR administrators, facilities teams, and engineering teams that need reliable attendance and presence data across distributed offices, field crews, and secure work sites.

## Product Purpose

Viso presents a high-precision attendance management platform: geofenced check-ins, identity verification, immutable logs, analytics, and developer integrations. Success means the interface feels trustworthy, fast to scan, and credible for organizations that rely on accurate workforce data.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise, premium. The product should feel like serious operational infrastructure rather than a playful consumer app.

## Anti-references

Avoid bright consumer dashboards, oversized decorative illustrations, generic blue SaaS gradients, playful gamified attendance screens, and cluttered HR portals that bury the operational signal.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with operational clarity: every section should make the business value obvious without excess copy.
2. Use accent color functionally: emerald marks action, verification, success, and active state.
3. Make data feel trustworthy: precise alignment, restrained borders, and compact metrics matter more than decoration.
4. Keep density controlled: show enough operational detail to feel real while preserving breathing room.
5. Design for administrators in flow: quick scanning, familiar controls, and minimal visual friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, preserve keyboard-visible focus states, avoid motion that blocks comprehension, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
